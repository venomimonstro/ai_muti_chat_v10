# Legal publication package

These files are product templates and launch-control artifacts, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

Before commercial launch, replace all `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` values, obtain counsel/accounting review where required, publish the final versions on the production domain, and record evidence in Admin Ops compliance signoffs.

Required public documents:

- `offer.md` — public offer / service terms;
- `privacy.md` — privacy and data-processing policy;
- `refunds.md` — payment/refund rules;
- `acceptable-use.md` — acceptable use and AI limitations.

The strict prelaunch check must remain blocked until `public-legal-documents`, `privacy-data-flow`, `wallet-fiscalization`, `receipt-refund-flow`, and `provider-commercial-terms` are explicitly approved with evidence references.
